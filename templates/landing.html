Legal [Redact]

A local privacy workstation for legal documents. It sorts files into matters, flags what could identify a person right beside the text, and prepares reviewed DOCX and PDF redactions with export checks.

Built for technically comfortable lawyers, legal-tech builders and developers evaluating a local review workflow, especially for Turkish legal documents.

Open the workstationRun it locally
  • Runs on your own computerOne command starts it. There is no hosted version and no upload service.
  • A person decides every findingNothing is removed until a reviewer approves it, or adds it themselves.
  • Detection by rules, not a modelRegular expressions, the official TCKN and VKN check digits, and a Turkish name gazetteer. The same document gives the same findings every time.
  • Reads what legal work usesDOCX, PDF, UDF, XLSX, PPTX, text, images and ZIP. Scans go through local OCR, used only once a reviewer accepts it. Redacted copies are built from DOCX and PDF.

Clearing it for an LLM is a separate question.

The reviewed copy reflects your decisions, a kept name included. Whether a document may go to an external LLM is decided separately.

The app clears a document for external LLM use only when all 8 of these hold. A person's approval is one of them, and nothing stands in for it. The app never sends the document anywhere itself.

As uploaded, before anyone has decided anything: Blocked: 5 of 8 conditions fail.

  • []Extraction complete, holdsComplete
  • []OCR accepted or not required, holdsnot required
  • []Document language is covered by the detection rules, holdsTurkish
  • []Residual risk after review is Low, failscurrently High
  • []No undecided critical findings, fails1 outstanding
  • []No direct identifiers remain, failsat least one remains
  • []Redaction pass completed, failsnot marked complete
  • []Human review approved, failsnot approved

What it does not do.

Read English well
The rules are tuned for Turkish. English name and address coverage is materially lower than Turkish coverage, so a document that does not read as Turkish is refused clearance. An English annex set out as labelled fields can still read as Turkish.
Call anything anonymous
Detection is not redaction, and redaction is not anonymization. Context can still identify a person or a matter after the obvious identifiers are gone.
Serve a team
One reviewer on one computer, bound to 127.0.0.1. There is no hosted version, and files sit unencrypted on local disk.

This page quotes no accuracy figure. Both of the project's test corpora are synthetic.
