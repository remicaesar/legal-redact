# Language gate: calibration history and rationale

The rules an agent must follow live in `AGENTS.md` under "Safety gates". This document is the measured history behind them — why each rule exists, what was tried and reverted, and the known limitations with their measurements. Read it before changing `legal_analyzer/classifier.py` language detection, `privacy.language_coverage()`, `privacy.language_evidence_spans`, the backfill, or `tests/test_language_gate.py`.

The gate clears only Turkish, and says so rather than clearing what it cannot read. `classifier.detect_language` derives TR / EN / Unknown from the extracted **text** — never the filename, which is what let 18 of 20 held-out English documents record as Turkish — and `privacy.language_coverage()` turns that into the gate's `language_supported` condition. Anything that is not TR, Unknown included, gets residual risk `Unknown`, `human_review_required` True and a refused gate; a short, empty or unidentifiable extraction is silence, and silence fails toward refusal, including a stored profile with no `language_coverage` key. Turkish is claimed only on Turkish marker **words** — never on letters — and only when the English in it is not **clustered**, so a Turkish filing carrying an English **prose** section is Unknown rather than measured by rules that find 38 of 76 English names. The measure is the longest run of English markers where Turkish evidence resets the run and markers more than `_ENGLISH_RUN_GAP` (4) words apart do not chain; `_MAX_ENGLISH_RUN` is 5, between a measured must-clear maximum of 2 and a must-refuse minimum of 9, and the band is identical at G = 3, 4, 6 and 8. **It measures position, not quantity, and that is the whole point** — two quantity rules shipped and both failed: a share of the evidence was diluted by padding the Turkish body (an English witness statement behind nine boilerplate clauses reached a 0.23 share and came back TR, gate allowed), and an absolute count refused ordinary Turkish filings as they lengthened (the same agreement clears at 100 words, refused at 300). A run is length-invariant: the agreement scores 2 at 100 and at 1000 words, the annex scores 12 behind 1, 9 or 28 clauses. Keep `_MIN_SIGNAL`; it is the only thing refusing the German contract (0 Turkish, 0 English). Known accepted edge: a Turkish contract quoting one long English clause verbatim scores 14 and is refused — a contiguous English section by this definition, failing in the safe direction. `IncidentalEnglishDoesNotRefuseATurkishFilingTests` and `TurkishCarryingEnglishIsNotClearedTests` both assert at several document lengths, because a single-size test cannot express length invariance — which is exactly how the count shipped. A previous round topped that floor up from the gazetteer and the TCKN check digits wherever there was **no English marker evidence**, to stop tabular Turkish being refused. It was reverted and must not come back in any form: an English document in labelled-field form carries no English *function words* either, so the condition was satisfied by an English employee data sheet listing Turkish parties — which then read as TR, cleared `language_supported`, and reached "Allowed for external LLM use after completed redaction and approval". Any rule keyed on the *absence* of English function words is open on exactly the shape that carries the most personal data per word. **A PROPER NOUN IS NOT A LANGUAGE.** The same defect arrived three times through the same door, each time keying the safety gate on personal data. (1) A Turkish PERSON name supplies ğ/ı/ş/İ/Ş, so an English witness-details form naming three Turkish people read as TR, hid the coverage notice and reached "Allowed for external LLM use" over a London address, a UK mobile, a national insurance number and a health disclosure. (2) Excluding `detect_person_names` spans closed only half: a wholly English form carrying `Şişli, İstanbul` and `Kadıköy` still read as TR with **zero findings**, residual Low, gate allowed. (3) Removing the letter path entirely left COMPANY names, because a Turkish legal form is literally made of marker words — "Demir Tekstil Sanayi **ve** Ticaret Anonim **Sirketi**" scores two, so naming one employer twice cleared the floor on an English data sheet that again reached Allowed.

So Turkish evidence is a Turkish marker WORD that falls OUTSIDE every detected entity span. `_marker_evidence(text, entity_spans)` takes the spans; `classifier.py` must never import `privacy.py` (privacy imports classifier — that is a cycle), so the caller passes them, both through `privacy.language_evidence_spans`: `analyze_privacy` with its deduped findings, `backfill_language_coverage` with the stored `risk_map`. That function also excludes the sweeps of the context vocabulary as it stood before `cezai şart`, confidentiality words and bare lawyer words stopped being triggers — a retired trigger removes a span, which can only ADD Turkish evidence, so it must not loosen the language signal. **Omitting spans counts MORE Turkish, so it is the permissive direction** — a caller that cannot supply them must not clear on the answer, which is why the backfill leaves a profile with no `risk_map` unanswered rather than settling it. **The span exclusion guards the Turkish arm ONLY, and that asymmetry is load-bearing.** English markers are counted wherever they fall, inside a detected entity or not. Dropping Turkish evidence can only REFUSE more; dropping English evidence can only CLEAR more. Applying the span test to both arms reopened the clustered-English route through the back door — the context sweeps are wide and trigger on English words (`privileged_or_confidential` covers 160 chars from "privileged"), so one span blanketed an English annex, erased 14 of its 17 English markers, cut the run from 14 to 2, and a Turkish filing carrying an unredacted London address, UK mobile and national insurance number reached "Allowed for external LLM use". `EnglishInsideADetectedEntityStillCountsTests` pins it, including the property that supplying spans never adds Turkish evidence and never removes English. Diacritic-bearing markers still count, because the list carries both spellings and the lookup strips U+0307 — verify that first if many fixtures suddenly fall. `AProperNounIsNotALanguageTests` pins all three routes and the whole consequence after a review pass; `TheBackfillSettlesStoredRecordsTests` pins that the backfill and detection agree. Do not reintroduce a letter-based path, and do not lower `_MIN_SIGNAL` or `_MIN_WORDS` to rescue what this costs: that threshold tuning has failed five times and reopens exactly this.

**The two marker lists must stay disjoint from the other language's ordinary words.** "her", "on", "not", "an", "at", "in", "as" and "it" were listed as English evidence, and a monolingual Turkish contract using "her iki taraf" and "on gün" was refused as unidentifiable. The 17 `tests/fixtures/*.txt` cannot catch that — not one of them contains a single English marker — so `OrdinaryTurkishIsNeverEnglishEvidenceTests` carries a Turkish document built from the colliding words, and pins that the corpus is silent on them.

**Known limitation — field-shaped English annexes read as Turkish.** The run measure counts consecutive English *function words*, so it sees English prose and not English *forms*. Measured on this tree: a 108-word document of Turkish boilerplate followed by an annex in labelled-field form — `Name: Marigold Quillfeather` / `Address line: 179 Cobblewell Street, London EC2A 4NE` / `Mobile: 07700 900318` / `National insurance number: QQ 12 34 56 C` / `Diagnosis: stress-related illness` / `Second witness: Desmond Crowhurst` — scores `_marker_evidence` (48, 2, 1) and returns TR, coverage supported. With both reviewer controls set it now produces four findings (both names, the address, the mobile), residual risk Medium and a blocked gate; but the national insurance number and the diagnosis are in no finding, so once a reviewer approves those four the gate reads "Allowed for external LLM use after completed redaction and approval." with both still in the text. Before the English rules it produced **zero findings** and was allowed outright. English prose trips the measure at 12; the same personal data as labelled fields trips it under 5, because `Name:`/`Address:`/`Mobile:` carry almost no function words — and a form is the shape that carries the most personal data per word. This is a limit of what a function-word signal can see, not a defect in the threshold, and re-tuning `_ENGLISH_RUN_GAP` or `_MAX_ENGLISH_RUN` does not reach it. The English detection rules narrowed it to the identifiers no rule detects.

**Known limitation — THE GATE IS CONSERVATIVE BUT NOT COMPLETE, and it refuses ordinary Turkish documents.** Turkish evidence is Turkish *words* outside every entity span, so a Turkish document whose Turkish content is mostly entity names — or one that is simply short — carries too little to clear `_MIN_SIGNAL` and comes back Unknown with the gate refusing. Measured on this tree, **five of the twenty-three Turkish gold fixtures are now refused**: `criminal_investigation.txt` (6 marker hits → 2), `medical_case.txt` (3 → 0), `ocr_scan_noisy.txt` (4 → 1), `sample_commercial_case.txt` (8 → 2) and `power_of_attorney.txt` (1, already refused). A six-row party roster and a 32-word ASCII roster are refused the same way, while `analyze_privacy` pulls eleven findings out of the latter — the Turkish rules read these perfectly and only the language signal cannot. **This is a refusal, not a leak**: the document is held back, nothing is cleared that should not be, and the cost is that a person reviews it.

Three things about this calibration, all of which must stay on the record:

* **There is NO HEADROOM.** Three clearing fixtures sit at exactly `_MIN_SIGNAL`: `court_judgment.txt`, `employment_dispute.txt` and `lease_agreement.txt`. One marker word fewer and they refuse. (`sample_pleading.txt` was the fourth until the prosecutor's-office rule stopped sweeping its clause, which put one more marker word outside every entity span.) `test_this_calibration_has_no_headroom` pins that set, so the next change to this signal needs re-measurement rather than judgement.
* **The absence of a known route is not evidence of safety.** The proper-noun class predicts more of itself: a court name, an institution, a ministry, a statute title all carry Turkish words and none of them has been separately measured. Three routes were found one at a time by looking; assume a fourth until someone has looked for it.
* **Three rescues were tried and reverted** — topping the floor up from the gazetteer and the TCKN check digits, counting Turkish letters, and narrowing that letter path to person names only — and each reopened a wholly English document clearing the gate.

`ATurkishDocumentWithoutFunctionWordsIsRefusedTests` and `AProperNounIsNotALanguageTests` pin the refusals; `test_every_turkish_gold_fixture_is_identified_as_turkish` names the five exceptions so a sixth falling is a failure rather than a drift, and it measures through the product path (`detected_language`, spans included) because a bare `detect_language` call reports a corpus that clears more widely than anything shipping does. The English detection rules did not change this — they add English findings and leave the language signal alone — and another narrowing here is not the fix.

**The four constants the signal turns on** live in `legal_analyzer/classifier.py`, each measured rather than chosen: `_MIN_WORDS` = 20 (below this many words there is not enough text to tell the languages apart, so the answer is Unknown rather than a guess), `_MIN_SIGNAL` = 3 (the marker evidence either language must clear before it is claimed at all — the only thing refusing the German contract, and the floor the three no-headroom fixtures sit exactly on), `_ENGLISH_RUN_GAP` = 4 (two English markers further apart than this do not chain, so scattered markers can never accumulate into a run) and `_MAX_ENGLISH_RUN` = 5 (the longest clustered English run a document may carry and still be claimed Turkish, between a measured must-clear maximum of 2 and a must-refuse minimum of 9). Do not lower the first two to rescue a refused Turkish document, and do not retune the last two to reach the field-shaped annex — neither is where the limit is.

**What `tests/test_language_gate.py` pins**, beyond the classes named above (`OrdinaryTurkishIsNeverEnglishEvidenceTests`, `IncidentalEnglishDoesNotRefuseATurkishFilingTests`, `ATurkishDocumentWithoutFunctionWordsIsRefusedTests`, `AProperNounIsNotALanguageTests`, `TurkishCarryingEnglishIsNotClearedTests`, `EnglishInsideADetectedEntityStillCountsTests`, `TheBackfillSettlesStoredRecordsTests`):

* `DetectLanguageReadsTheDocumentTests` — the signal is the extracted text and the filename cannot reach it, in either direction, including Turkish typed without diacritics; it also carries the `tests/fixtures` calibration (`test_every_turkish_gold_fixture_is_identified_as_turkish`, `test_the_refused_fixtures_are_refused_for_want_of_turkish_words`, `test_this_calibration_has_no_headroom`).
* `ALanguageTheRulesCannotReadIsNotTurkishTests` — the German contract, which supplies ç/ö/ü and no Turkish word, is refused by the ordinary rule rather than a special case, and a full review pass does not open its gate.
* `EnglishDocumentIsNotClearedTests` — the reported reproduction re-run with both reviewer controls set: the English rules now report its name, address, mobile, dates and amount, but residual risk is Unknown, the gate refused, `human_review_required` True, and the readiness line stating a limit rather than an error.
* `UnknownLanguageRefusesTests` — silence fails toward refusal: an empty extraction, a fragment under `_MIN_WORDS`, a stored profile with no `language_coverage` key, and a language code that is not the exact one `detect_language` emits.
* `TurkishIsUnchangedTests` — the control: a Turkish petition is still measured, reaches `EXTERNAL_LLM_ALLOWED` when fully reviewed, and its `language_supported` condition passes with detail "Turkish".
* `StoredLanguageComesFromTheDocumentTests` — end to end over the upload path: `documents.language` is written from the gate's decision and cannot disagree with it across a refresh.
* `ALanguageRefusalIsVisibleWhereRefusalsAreCountedTests` — a language refusal reads as Blocked where refusals are counted and filtered, so it is not the one refusal a reviewer filtering for blocked documents never sees.
* `AcceptedOcrCarriesItsOwnLanguageAnswerTests` — a document whose text came from reviewer-accepted OCR is answered from that accepted text by the backfill and by nothing else, and stays refused until the backfill runs.
* `AStoredClearanceIsNotServedAsClearanceTests` — every read surface computes the gate instead of deserializing a cached `allowed: true`, and reading a document does not rewrite it.

**Known limitation — the language statement lives in five places and must be consolidated upward, not downward.** The same claim is written as `LANGUAGE_COVERAGE_CONDITION`, `EXTERNAL_LLM_LANGUAGE_BLOCKED`, `_LANGUAGE_LIMIT`, `coverage["summary"]` and the string `renderLanguageCoverageNotice` builds in `templates/studio.html`. The browser copy is deliberately the **fullest** — it alone says "no model reads the document", says the release gate stays closed, tells the reviewer to read the document and record what the rules did not reach, and states "English name and address coverage is materially lower than Turkish coverage" in the exact words README.md uses and `tests/test_trust_surfaces.py` pins. Rendering `coverage["summary"]` there instead was measured and rejected: it deletes the first three and weakens the fourth to "Coverage outside Turkish is materially lower". That duplication is why the notice test pins template source. If this is ever consolidated, move the FULLEST wording into the shared source and render that — do not adopt the shortest. Adding the English detection rules did not consolidate it.

**Known limitation — on an un-backfilled corpus the risk badge lags the status.** `residual_risk` is deliberately read from the stored column rather than derived (it is the detection-time measurement the accuracy audit is calibrated on), and nothing runs the backfill automatically — `ensure_db_ready()` applies migrations only, and `docker/bootstrap.py` does not call it. So until `python3 db/backfill_language_coverage.py` is run, an already-indexed document can render a green `Low` in the risk column directly beside a `Blocked` status, in the dashboard table (`templates/index.html`) and the matter workspace (`templates/matter.html`), and `?risk=Low` still returns it. **This is a cosmetic stale cache, not a gate failure**: the release gate and every export path derive their answer on read and refuse the document correctly. Running the documented backfill resolves it. Not wiring that into startup is deliberate containment — it re-extracts every source file, which is not work to do inside a first HTTP request. The English detection rules did not change it.

`privacy_profile["language_coverage"]` is the **detection-time** answer and is written only where the text was actually read (`analyze_privacy`, the OCR accept path, the backfill). `refresh_release_state` never sees text, so it decides on an absent key as uncovered but must **not** write that decision back onto the profile: doing so made an absent answer indistinguishable from a detected Unknown and stranded every legacy document that any review, OCR or PDF write path touched before the backfill could claim it — permanently refused, recoverable only by `apply_reextraction()`, which discards the reviewer decisions the backfill exists to preserve. The decision it reached lives on the gate it decided (`external_llm_gate["language_coverage"]`), and that is what `refresh_document_state` and the review UPDATE in `blueprints/review.py` write `documents.language` from. Only the exact code `detect_language` emits counts — `'tr'` is not Turkish here. The stored profile is a *cache* of a gate decision, and one written under an earlier condition list still claims `allowed: true`, so `/api/document/<id>` computes the release state through `released_state_for()` instead of deserializing it; `refresh_document_state` persists what that same function returns. **Every** read surface derives it the same way — `/api/documents` and `/api/matter/<id>` both call `released_state_for()` per row, which is why the `readiness` filter and pagination run in Python rather than SQL. Do not put any of them back on the stored column. What the cache still feeds is `residual_risk`, which is deliberately not derived (it is the detection-time measurement), so an existing corpus is settled once by `python3 db/backfill_language_coverage.py` (`backfill_language_coverage` in `app.py`) — a Python one-off, not a `.sql` migration, because a migration may not touch `privacy_profile`. That backfill is the **only** place the language answer is recovered for a stored profile: a second copy ran on every read for OCR-accepted documents, could flip one from refused to cleared without writing anything, and was removed rather than kept in sync. It settles coverage, and residual risk **only where coverage fails** (a document the rules cannot read has no measured level, and a leftover Low badge is the same false green light in another column) — on a covered document the stored level is the detection-time figure the accuracy audit is calibrated on, and recomputing it there would silently re-measure the document against whatever its source file says today. It must never become `apply_reextraction()`, which resets the release-control booleans and replaces findings. A document whose text it cannot read is left **entirely alone** — no `language_coverage` key and no residual-risk rewrite. The gate reads an absent key as uncovered either way, so nothing is cleared; writing either field is a one-way door. The coverage key makes the skip at the top of the loop pass over that document forever, and the risk rewrite is never undone by the supported branch, so a corpus backfilled from a container that could not see the source paths would have come back Unknown-badged and stayed that way even after a successful second run. Every refusal `external_llm_gate_policy()` can return must start with "Blocked", because the dashboard's Blocked chip selects on that prefix (in Python, over the derived value, case-insensitively) and `benchmark.py` and `classify.py` report on it the same way. The SQL `LIKE 'Blocked%'` KPI that used to be the reason was deleted: it read the cached column, nothing rendered it, and deriving it would have added another full-corpus pass to a page that already makes several. `tests/test_language_gate.py` pins the refusal, the Turkish control and the `tests/fixtures` calibration; English detection rules exist but do not feed the gate — do not read the refusal as coverage, or the English findings as coverage either.
